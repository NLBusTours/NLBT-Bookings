Built from the ground up

One woman. One big idea. A whole lot of hard work.

NL Bus Tours was founded by Eliza when she was just 27 years old. What started as one ambitious idea became a business she built through determination, creativity and countless hours of hard work.

Eliza saw potential where others might have seen an old school bus. She imagined something different: giving hardworking buses a second chance and turning ordinary journeys into experiences people would remember. Making that vision real meant taking risks, learning as she went, solving problems and putting in the work, even when the road was anything but easy.

From the first bus to the fleet you see today, NL Bus Tours reflects her resourcefulness, independence and refusal to give up on an idea she believed in. Every transformation, every new chapter and every memory made along the way is part of the story she started.

And that spirit still drives the business today: making the journey just as memorable as the destination.

The originals

The buses that started it all.

These retired favourites helped shape NL Bus Tours. They're part of our history, even though they're no longer available to book.

The first bus · Retired

Artie

Our first bus, a former Blue Bird school bus, was transformed into a colourful space for music, laughter and adventures. Artie's name was inspired by Articuno, the big blue bird Pokémon.

The second bus · Retired

Archie

Archie followed Artie, bringing lounge seating, a dance floor and a personality of his own. His Newfoundland-inspired artwork celebrated the place we call home.
